ARTIST STATEMENT

My artistic practice consists of oil painting, woodcut printmaking, and digital 3D modelling, with painting being the space where I find myself most often.

Working primarily from photographs I take myself, I collect fragments of my surroundings and reinterpret them through painting. Rather than attempting to faithfully reproduce an image, I allow the process to develop intuitively, leaving room for personal interpretation, change, and uncertainty.

A recurring characteristic of my paintings is their unfinished state. For a long time, I regarded this as a weakness in my practice, something that reflected an inability to bring a work to completion. Over time, however, I began to reconsider what it means for something to be finished.

I have come to understand this incompleteness as something closely connected to my experience of life itself. Things do not always arrive at a clear conclusion, and perhaps they do not need to. What I once considered a shortcoming has gradually become a meaningful part of my visual language.

My work exists somewhere between the desire to create something complete and the acceptance of leaving things unresolved. Perhaps my paintings are not unfinished after all. Perhaps they simply end where I no longer need to continue.
